OUR TRIPS

Pick a trip that matches your experience, from beginner to intermediate to advanced to expert.

Half Day Adventure

A gentle, scenic float on calm rapids. Perfect for families and anyone new to rafting.

Duration: 4 hours

Difficulty: Beginner

Big Mallard

Steady waves and bright turquoise water keep the whole crew paddling in rhythm. A good step up once you've tried a beginner trip.

Duration: 4 hours

Difficulty: Intermediate

Serpent's Bend

This short run twists through tight curves where teamwork matters more than strength. Your guide will call the turns.

Duration: 2 hours

Difficulty: Intermediate

Los Dientes del Diablo

A long day of fast current and big waves for rafters who already have river experience.

Duration: 6 hours

Difficulty: Advanced

Devil's Gate Run

Our toughest and longest trip, with huge rapids and narrow channels. Only for confident, experienced paddlers.

Duration: 8 hours

Difficulty: Expert
